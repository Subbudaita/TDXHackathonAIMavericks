Cloud Realtors leverages Data Cloud to ingest and unify leads and lead engagement activity from different public-facing websites and campaigns and activates directly into Salesforce, where our Agentforce AI Voice Assistant makes up to three intelligently timed calls to qualify requirements—location, budget, property type—and passes detailed leads to human SDRs for personalized email follow-up and tailored brochure delivery. Once a prospect expresses interest, the Sales Rep converts the lead into an Opportunity, requests and secures automatic discount approval via Slack, and instantly generates a fully customized rental agreement. Clients then access our public portal’s “My Properties” dashboard to review listings, while our Service Agent manages move-in checklists and utility transfers—integrated with third-party vendors for real-time status updates. With additional development time, we’d introduce an eco-score calculation that evaluates properties based on market rate, age, location, and sustainability factors, and automatically assign the most suitable agent by pincode.
